A&A Promotion — Website Brief

Prepared by: Andrea Chisholm and Audrey Williams
Business: A&A Promotion
Date: ____________________

1. About A&A Promotion

A&A Promotion is an events business run by Andrea Chisholm and Audrey Williams. We organise enjoyable, memorable experiences that bring people together.

Our events have included Caribbean brunches, polo experiences and social outings. We are also exploring new experiences, including wine and gin days and other special events.

We would like a professional website that reflects our business and helps us reach both existing and new customers.

2. Purpose of the website

We would like the website to:

* Introduce A&A Promotion and explain what we offer.
* Showcase upcoming events and share highlights from past events.
* Give customers clear information about each event.
* Make it easy for customers to contact us, express interest or book.
* Help us grow our customer database and keep people informed about future events.
* Present A&A Promotion as a welcoming, professional and trustworthy business.

3. Our customers

Our website will be aimed at adults interested in social events, celebrations and group experiences. Many of our customers currently hear about our events through personal recommendations, WhatsApp and social media.

We would like the website to be easy to use, particularly on a mobile phone.

4. Suggested website pages

We would welcome your advice on the most suitable layout, but envisage the following pages:

Home — An introduction to A&A Promotion, with featured events and clear links to find out more or book.

About Us — Information about Andrea and Audrey, the business and the experiences we offer.

Upcoming Events — Details of forthcoming events, including dates, venues, prices, what is included and how to book.

Past Events and Gallery — Photographs and highlights from previous events, with customer feedback where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions — Information about bookings, deposits, payment plans, cancellations and other common questions.

Contact Us — A contact form and links to our social media. We would also like to discuss whether a WhatsApp contact button would be suitable.

5. Event bookings and payments

We would like to explore options for customers to:

* Book and pay for an event online.
* Pay a deposit where this is offered.
* Pay the remaining balance at a later date, including instalments if possible.
* Receive a booking confirmation and payment receipt.
* See the terms and conditions before booking.
* Join a waiting list if an event is fully booked.

Please explain which of these features you recommend, how they would work and any additional costs or transaction fees. We would also like to know how we would view and manage bookings.

6. Mailing list and customer information

We would like visitors to be able to sign up to hear about future A&A Promotion events.

Please explain how the mailing list would work, how we would obtain and manage customers’ permission to receive updates, and how we could access or export our customer information if we changed providers.

7. Look and feel

We would like the website to feel:

* Stylish and professional.
* Warm, welcoming and easy to navigate.
* Suitable for promoting special events and experiences.
* Visually appealing on both phones and computers.

We can share our logo, flyers and examples of designs or websites we like.

Preferred colours or style: ______________________________

Examples of websites we like: ___________________________

8. Content and photographs

We expect to provide some existing materials, which may include our logo, event flyers, photographs and information about previous events.

Please let us know:

* What content you would need from us.
* Whether you can help with writing or editing the website text.
* Whether you can help select and prepare photographs.
* Whether any images, design work or content creation would cost extra.

We will discuss which photographs and customer comments we have permission to use.

9. Managing the website

We would like both Audrey’s and Andrea  to be able to access and manage the website.

Please explain how easily we could:

* Add or update events, prices, photographs and text.
* View bookings and customer enquiries.
* Access the mailing list.
* Make routine changes ourselves.

Please also let us know what training and written guidance you provide.

10. Domain, ownership and ongoing support

We would like to understand:

* Who will register and own the domain name. We have one already 
* Who will own the website design, content and customer database.
* Whether the website can be moved to another provider in the future.
* What hosting, security, backups and maintenance are included.
* What support is available after the website launches.
* How much ongoing services and future changes will cost.
