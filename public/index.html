FAQ

Good to know

Can't see your question? Get in touch.

Who are your events for?

Adults who enjoy social events, celebrations and group experiences.

How do I book?

Choose an event under What's on and book online. Your confirmation and receipt arrive by email.

Can I pay a deposit?

Where an event offers a deposit, you'll see it on the event card, and you can pay the balance at a later date.

Can I spread my payment?

Where an event offers a payment plan, it's shown on the event card before you book.

What if I need to cancel?

Each event's terms and conditions, including cancellations, are shown before you pay.

What if an event is sold out?

Join the waiting list on the event card. If a place opens up, we'll let you know.

How do I hear about new events?

Join the guest list, or follow us on Instagram at @aandapromotion.
